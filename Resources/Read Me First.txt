ANVIL — play Windows games from Steam and Epic on your Mac
Made by Jay Chirag Patel · Santa Clara, California

INSTALLING
1. Drag Anvil into the Applications folder.
2. Open Anvil from Applications.
   If macOS says it "cannot verify" Anvil: open System Settings › Privacy & Security,
   scroll down, and choose "Open Anyway" next to Anvil. You only do this once.

WHAT YOU NEED
- A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.
- Rosetta. If it's missing, Anvil's setup tells you how to add it.
- About 2 GB free for Anvil's engine and Steam, plus room for your games.

THE FIRST START
Anvil's setup tests your Mac's graphics, downloads the game engine (about 200 MB, from
its publisher), and offers to set up Steam (from Valve) and sign in to Epic Games. Your
passwords go only to Steam's and Epic's own sign-in pages, never to Anvil.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Games with kernel anti-cheat (Riot Vanguard, EA Javelin, and some others) can't run
  their online modes on a Mac. Anvil marks those games. Easy Anti-Cheat and BattlEye
  games work online only where the game's maker allows Wine.
- Anvil tunes each game as you play it. Give a new game a few minutes.
